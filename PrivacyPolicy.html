Buxton Team Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is important to us

Effective Date: May 23, 2025
We offer you the opportunity to search for homes on our website for free. If you would like to receive MLS listing updates or utilize certain features, we will need to collect some information. This policy outlines the information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your information.
1. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide and improve our services to you. This includes:
1.1. Information You Provide Directly:
Phone Number: We collect your phone number to provide you with updated listing information, messages to inform you of upcoming events, and to follow-up with you regarding showings and appointments.
Other Information: We may also collect other personal information you voluntarily provide, such as your name and email address, when you contact us or use specific services.
1.2. Website Usage Data and Google Analytics:
Like many websites, we use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors engage with our website. Google Analytics is a web analytics service offered by Google that tracks and reports website traffic.
Data Collected: Google Analytics uses "cookies," which are small text files placed on your computer, to collect standard internet log information (such as your IP address, browser type, operating system) and visitor behavior information (such as pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked). We have configured Google Analytics to anonymize IP addresses as soon as data is received by the Analytics Collection Network, before any storage or processing occurs.
Purpose of Collection: We use the aggregated and anonymized information gathered by Google Analytics to understand website traffic and webpage usage, improve our website's functionality and content, and for internal analytical purposes. This helps us to better understand our visitors' needs and to optimize our service and user experience.
Google's Data Use: Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics about your visits to this site is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data here: Google Partner Site Policies.
Opting Out of Google Analytics: You can prevent your data from being collected and used by Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available at: Google Opt-Out Analytics Add-on. Using this add-on will not affect your ability to use our website.
Cookies: Our website uses cookies (small text files stored on your device) to enhance your experience, remember your preferences, and for analytics purposes as described above. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
Provide, operate, and maintain our website and services.
Communicate with you, including sending you notifications about listings, our monthly newsletter, invitations to our events, updates about the real estate market, and responding to your inquiries.
Personalize and improve your experience on our website.
Analyze usage and trends to improve our services.
Comply with legal obligations.
3. Information Sharing
We will not share your personal information, such as your phone number or email address, or message content with third parties, except under the following circumstances:
With Your Consent: We may share your information with third parties if you have given us your explicit consent to do so.
To Comply with the Law: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).
Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our services (such as website hosting, data analytics, email delivery, and customer service).
Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal information may be transferred.
4. Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
5. Your Choices
Marketing Communications: You can opt-out of receiving marketing messages from us at any time by replying "STOP" to a text message or by using the "unsubscribe" link provided in our emails.
Access and Deletion: You can request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details provided below. We will respond to your request in accordance with applicable law.
Cookies and Analytics: As mentioned in section 1.2, you can control cookies through your browser settings and opt-out of Google Analytics tracking.
6. Changes to This Policy:
We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.
