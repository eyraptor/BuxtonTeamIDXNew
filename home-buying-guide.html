How We Guide You Through Every Step

We've developed a comprehensive approach to home buying that addresses every aspect of your journey. We maintain relationships with a list of trusted lenders, so if you haven't already chosen one, we can connect you with professionals who understand your financial goals and will work to get you pre-approved quickly. Once we understand your timeline and budget, we dive deep into understanding your lifestyle needs and preferences—your must-haves, deal-breakers, and the neighborhoods that match your vision. Our expertise in market negotiations gives us an advantage when making offers and working through the entire buying process. When inspections happen, we guide you through the results and use our negotiation skills to advocate for fair repair requests or credits from the seller. From start to closing, you'll have experienced professionals in your corner.

The Buxton Team 10-Point Buying System

1

Choose the Buxton Team

Partner with us to guide you through every step of the home buying process. We'll be with you from start to finish.

2

Choose Your Lender

We can suggest trusted lenders who have helped our home buyers get pre-approved quickly and understand your financial goals.

3

Decide on Neighborhood & Area

Let's explore neighborhoods that match your lifestyle. We know the OKC market inside and out and can guide you to the right community for your needs.

4

Decide on a Price Point

Based on your pre-approval and financial situation, let's determine the right price range for your home search.

5

Decide on a Time Frame

Understanding your timeline helps us find the right home at the right time and prepare you for the buying process ahead.

6

Prepare Earnest Money

Typically 1% of the purchase price is necessary for earnest money. We'll help you prepare and ensure you're ready when you find the perfect home.

7

View Homes in Desired Area

We'll schedule showings and help you evaluate homes. Remember, sellers often have hidden cameras inside and outside—we'll guide you on what to discuss during negotiations.

8

Make Comparables

We'll do market comparables to ensure we negotiate properly and you're getting a fair price for the home you want.

9

Connect to Proper Vendors

We'll connect you with trusted home inspectors, termite inspectors, and other vendors you'll need throughout the buying process.

10

Choose a Home Insurance Agent

We can recommend trusted insurance agents who will help you protect your new home investment with the right coverage.

Brent and Edna will help you through the entire process. We take everything one step at a time with you. It is always your final choice for homes to view and vendors you will use. We are always there for helpful suggestions and to support you all the way to when you get your new keys and beyond.

Special Services for Special Situations

Relocating to OKC?

Moving to a new city is exciting but challenging. We help out-of-state relocators navigate the OKC market, understand different neighborhoods, and find a home that feels like home. We can connect you with moving companies, utility providers, and community resources to make your transition smooth.

First-Time Home Buyer?

First-time home buying can feel overwhelming. We specialize in guiding new buyers through every step—from understanding pre-approval and down payment options to negotiating and closing. We'll explain everything in plain language and ensure you feel confident in your decisions.

Investment Property?

Growing your real estate portfolio requires expertise. We have extensive experience helping investors find properties with strong appreciation potential and positive cash flow. We understand rental markets, property management considerations, and investment strategies specific to Oklahoma City.
